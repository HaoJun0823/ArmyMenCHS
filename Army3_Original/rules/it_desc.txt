When a sarge is killed by an enemy he
becomes "it". He remains "it" until the
next sarge is killed by an enemy. That
sarge then becomes "it".
While a player is "it" all players in
other teams score 10 pts every 3 seconds.

The "it" sarge is indicated by a square on
the strategic map.

The first player/team to reach the point
goal wins.

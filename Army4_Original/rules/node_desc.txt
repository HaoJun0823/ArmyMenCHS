Control of as many of the nodes as
possible. You take control of a node when
your forces get within range of it and no
other forces are in range. You keep
control until another army takes control
of the node.

Score 4 pts every 10 seconds for 1 node,
12 pts every 10 seconds for 2 nodes,
24 pts every 10 seconds for 3 nodes,
40 pts every 10 seconds for 4 nodes,
60 pts every 10 seconds for 5 nodes.

The first player/team to reach the point
goal wins.

Bringing all of the enemy flags to home
base scores 1000 pts. They may be
brought to home base one at a time but no
points are scored until all enemy flags
have been captured and returned to home
base.

A flag returns to its base if unheld for 2
minutes.

The first player/team to reach the point
goal wins. So when playing to the default
goal of 1000 pts capturing all enemy flags
wins the game.
